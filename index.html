About the game

SpaceShooter Party is an arcade arena dogfight game for up to 4 players. Each player controls a neon triangle ship with just two buttons: one to rotate and boost, one to fire.

Ships crack after one hit and are destroyed after two, leaving a tiny pilot floating in space who respawns after a few seconds. Blast special asteroids to collect power-ups and turn the fight around:

  • Laser
  • Shield
  • Saber
  • Bomb

Features

  • 8 unique mapsEach arena plays differently.
  • BotsThree difficulty levels to practise against or fill empty seats.
  • Local party modeUp to 4 players on one device.
  • Online playJoin friends with a simple room code.
  • CollectiblesUnlock ship skins and pilots.
  • TutorialLearn the controls in a minute.
