Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

SpaceShooter Party (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is developed by Light Studio, based in Indonesia. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

Contact: lightdevs2005@gmail.com

2. No sign-up required

When you first open the game, it automatically creates an anonymous guest account for you. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or postal address.

3. Data we store on our game server

To run your account, online matches and rewards, our server stores:

  • Account identifiers: a random account ID and a secret account token. The token itself is stored on your device; our server stores only a hashed (one-way scrambled) version of it.
  • Display name: the in-game name you choose (length is limited).
  • In-game progress: coin balance, owned and equipped ship skins and pilots, match and win counts, daily reward counters, and which redeem codes you have used.
  • Offline match results submitted for coin rewards: win/loss, number of rounds, opponent type, and match duration.
  • Technical connection data: your IP address and connection quality data (such as ping and packet loss). This is used temporarily to run online matches, prevent abuse and fix network problems. Server logs are rotated and kept only briefly.

4. Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game may sign you in automatically to link your progress to your Play Games account. This lets you restore your progress after reinstalling the game or on a new device.

  • We receive your Play Games player ID and Play Games display name.
  • We do not receive your email address or password.
  • The game uses the Google scopes games, games_lite and drive.appdata. The drive.appdata scope gives access only to a private, app-specific storage area used solely for game data. We cannot see your other Google Drive files.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (which you can choose to watch for bonus coins) and occasional interstitial (full-screen) ads.

To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers such as your Advertising ID, your IP address, and ad interaction data. This collection is performed by Google under its own policies. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Players who purchase “Remove Ads” are not shown interstitial ads.

6. In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. We only receive a purchase confirmation needed to deliver the item you bought.

7. Data stored on your device

The game stores the following locally on your device:

  • Game settings
  • A local copy of your progress
  • Your account token
  • A queue of offline match results waiting to be synced to our server

The game does not use cookies or third-party analytics trackers of its own. (Google AdMob and Google Play services work as described above.)

8. How we use data

We use the data described above only to:

  • run the game and online matches;
  • keep your progress and purchases;
  • calculate and protect coin rewards (anti-cheat checks and daily limits);
  • show ads;
  • provide support; and
  • find and fix bugs.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to the game.

9. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers needed to run the game:

  • Google — Play Games Services, Play Billing and AdMob.
  • Hostinger — our server hosting provider. Our servers are located in Malaysia.

We may also disclose data if required to do so by law.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • Server backups are kept for up to 14 days.
  • Server logs containing connection data are rotated and kept only briefly.
  • Inactive guest accounts may be deleted after a long period of inactivity.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data: data is transmitted over encrypted connections where supported, account tokens are stored only in hashed form on our server, and access to our servers is restricted. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the account data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Email lightdevs2005@gmail.com with your in-game name and, if possible, the account ID shown in the game's Profile/Settings. We will respond within 30 days.

13. How to delete your data

To delete your account data from our server:

  1. Open SpaceShooter Party and note your in-game name and, if possible, the account ID shown in Profile/Settings.
  2. Send an email to lightdevs2005@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”, including your in-game name and account ID.
  3. We will confirm and delete your account data (account ID, hashed token, display name, progress, match results and any linked Play Games player ID) within 30 days.

Deleted data may remain in server backups for up to 14 days before it is permanently removed. Deletion is permanent: coins, skins, pilots and purchases tied to the account cannot be restored afterwards.

Data on your device: uninstalling the app (or clearing the app's data in Android settings) deletes the data stored on your device.

Advertising data: data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

14. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at lightdevs2005@gmail.com and we will remove it.

15. International users

Your data may be processed in Malaysia (where our servers are located) and in other countries where Google operates. These countries may have data protection laws different from those in your country.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the game or on this page.

17. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact Light Studio at lightdevs2005@gmail.com.
